Nothing shows until the store picks a product for ITS domain (the picks in settings_data.json are other stores'). Lines are matched to a pick by productId (the `upsell` flag is not saved in localStorage), so a product you SELL picked there was taken for protection and Add to cart emptied the cart (live 2026-11-23, no JS error, drawer at $0.00). A protection product named otherwise is ignored (fail-safe): widen the regex there.

Lines show colour / size as one box "White, M ▾" (`.lu-vchg` = [data-lu-vchg], toggles line.__open, not saved) over the selects; option names appear only in its aria-label, the store's option "Side" read "Size" there (LN in FREE_LINE), never renamed in the data (checkout matches variants by id and names). A line without selects shows its values only (`.lu-vval` spans, ", " by CSS). Gift lines (D.gift, or all units free) get a "Free gift" chip appended to the title span (.lu-gift-badge, --vn-hl tint) and no price (.regular-price display:none).
